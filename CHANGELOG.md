## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-02

### Added

- 17 themes and 10 layouts distilled from real App Store and Google Play listings.
- `render` exports pixel-exact images for `ios-6.9`, `ios-6.5` and `android-phone`, as PNG without an alpha channel or as JPEG.
- `preview` and `gallery` commands to review a set and compare it across every theme.
- `capture` for the iOS Simulator and Android emulators, with a clean 9:41 status bar.
- `ai plan`, `ai translate` and `ai asset` with Claude, OpenAI, Gemini and Grok.
- Free asset sources: Microsoft Fluent 3D icons and Notionists illustrations.
- JSON Schema for `shotforge.json`.
- Example project in `examples/lumen`.
